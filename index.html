Educational task · Selective attention

Stroop Test

This activity explores selective attention by asking you to identify the ink colour of a colour word, rather than read the word itself.

What to do

For each trial, click the button matching the colour of the letters. Work as accurately and quickly as you comfortably can. There are 60 trials in a mixed order: 30 congruent and 30 incongruent trials, interleaved throughout the activity.

In congruent trials, the word and ink colour match. In incongruent trials, they differ. The task records response time and incorrect responses for each condition.

Privacy and scope

This is a non-diagnostic educational activity, not a clinical or intelligence test. Your entries and results remain in this browser session; this app does not send them to a server. Share a screenshot only if you choose to. You may stop at any time.
